Swing Trading

Focus on daily and weekly trends, key levels, catalysts, and reward relative to risk.

Core checklist

✓ Higher-timeframe trend and structure
✓ Supply/demand and liquidity levels
✓ Upcoming earnings and macro catalysts
✓ Volume, relative strength, and risk/invalidation

Research tools

Technical patterns, moving averages, candlestick confirmation, institutional filing trends, and event reaction history.

Typically 1–2 years

LEAPS Trading

Assess company fundamentals and long-term thesis alongside option sensitivity and expiration risk.

Core checklist

✓ Revenue and earnings outlook
✓ Competitive moat and valuation
✓ Delta, implied volatility and Vega
✓ Time remaining, break-even and exit plan

Beginner explanations

Delta: How sensitive the option is to a $1 stock move.

Vega: How sensitive the option is to volatility changes.

IV: The market’s estimate of potential future movement, not direction.

Live options chains and Greeks require an external data provider.
Multi-year horizon

Long-Term Investing

Prioritize business quality, moat durability, management, cash generation and valuation.

Core checklist

✓ Wide or improving moat supported by evidence
✓ Durable growth and healthy cash flow
✓ Responsible capital allocation
✓ Valuation and margin of safety

Business-first research

Review company segments, executive track records, competitors, financial red flags, and ETFs.
