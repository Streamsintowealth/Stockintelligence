Financial terms made simple

Competitive moat

Financial: sustainable competitive advantage. Everyday: what makes it hard for competitors to steal the company's customers or profits.

Price-to-earnings (P/E)

Financial: stock price divided by earnings per share. Everyday: how much you're paying for each dollar the business earns.

Free cash flow

Financial: operating cash flow minus capital expenditures. Everyday: money left after the company pays for the investments needed to run its business.

Implied volatility

Financial: option-implied expectation of future volatility. Everyday: how much movement options prices are reflecting, not whether the stock goes up or down.
